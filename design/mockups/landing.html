SIEM Investigation Lab

A clear safety boundary

  • Every log, host, person and incident is fictional.
  • External addresses come only from ranges reserved for documentation.
  • Nothing in the platform scans, connects to or attacks a real system.
  • Every exercise states its objective, environment and restrictions.
